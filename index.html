Why It Matters

Landon was fortunate to experience Portugal once before through soccer, and that trip gave him a chance to train, learn, and see the game from a different perspective.

This trip is different. In just a few weeks, he will travel with Indy Eleven Academy to compete in IberCup Estoril, an international tournament featuring teams from around the world.

This trip is about competition, growth, and representing his club on an international stage. For Landon, it is another step forward and a chance to test himself in a tournament environment against players and teams he would not normally get to face.
